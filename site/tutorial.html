Welcome to Enginuity Beta

Enginuity Beta helps you turn everyday materials into real engineering builds. SparkAI guides you with steps, diagrams, progress tracking, and a helper you can ask when you get stuck.

The three modes

  • Apprentice — SparkAI leads step by step. Best if you are new.
  • Associate — You and SparkAI build together. Good when you want more control.
  • Innovator — You lead the idea; SparkAI supports when you need it.

Your first project: Innovator Lite

You will start with Innovator Lite — a short 15–20 minute build using items you already have at home. You will list your materials, generate a project, follow the steps, try a visual diagram, track progress, and ask SparkHelper questions.

Safety
Do not build anything dangerous (fire, explosives, toxic chemicals, high voltage). If a step feels unsafe, stop and pick a safer version.
