Your first build (guided)

Enginuity has three modes. For your first project, we’ll use Apprentice mode so SparkAI guides you step-by-step.

  • Apprentice: SparkAI guides you step by step and explains everything. Best for beginners learning the basics.
  • Associate: SparkAI is still holding the wheel, but you make more choices, have more authority. Good if you already know a little.
  • Innovator: You lead the project, SparkAI supports you when needed. Best for creative or advanced users.
Safety note
Please don’t try to build anything dangerous (fire, explosives, toxic chemicals, high voltage). If a project feels unsafe, stop and choose a safer version.
