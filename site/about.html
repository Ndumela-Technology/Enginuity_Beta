About Enginuity

Building intelligent AI tools that help people think, create, and innovate.

Powered by SparkAI

Our Mission

Enginuity was founded by Bonolo Ndumela in August 2026. Bonolo is a South African engineering student studying in the Netherlands with a passion for technology, innovation, and artificial intelligence. Powered by SparkAI, Enginuity was created to make advanced AI more practical, approachable, and useful for everyone. Our goal is to build AI that doesn't simply answer questions, it helps people think, create, solve problems, and bring ideas to life.

Whether someone is developing a business, learning a new skill, researching a topic, or exploring their next idea, Spark is designed to become a trusted AI partner.

What We Offer

Innovator

AI-powered idea generation, brainstorming, planning, and strategy tools designed to help transform ideas into action.

Associate

Everyday AI assistance for productivity, research, writing, organisation, and problem solving.

Apprentice

Fully guided, step-by-step project support that helps users build confidence while learning practical engineering and problem-solving skills.

Our Vision

We believe artificial intelligence should empower people, not replace them. Spark is being built to create intelligent tools that work alongside individuals, creators, students, entrepreneurs, and professionals.

Built for the Future

Spark continues to evolve through user feedback and innovation. Every improvement is designed to make the platform smarter, faster, and more useful while maintaining a simple experience.
